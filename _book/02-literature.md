# Literature

We should not trust internal meta-analysis because of the following assumptions:

1.  No small studies were p-hacked: p-hacked share consistent bias.
2.  The meta-analysis consists of only valid studies: authors could selectively choose studies with favorable results.

More info can be found on [Data Colada](https://datacolada.org/73) or formal journal articles [@Vosgerau_2019] and [@Simmons_2011]
